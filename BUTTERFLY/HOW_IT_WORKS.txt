HOW IT WORKS - Butterfly
JIN2 · @jin_22ee

The whole artwork is one Python file. Something strikes a glass wall
from behind until it breaks, and butterflies come out of the hole.
Every part of it is made of letters.

1. Wings
   A forewing and a hindwing are written down as outlines. Random points
   are kept only if they fall inside, so each wing is a cloud of points
   in its real shape. Points near the body are brighter.

2. Flight
   The wings rise and fall on each butterfly's own beat. The body dips a
   little as the wings come down, faces its back toward you so the wings
   read clearly, and leans into a turn.

3. Paths
   Twenty-two butterflies, each with a home position at its own depth.
   They wander in slow loops around it, wide enough to leave the frame
   and come back later. Far ones are smaller and dimmer. Each one starts
   small at the hole and grows as it squeezes out.

4. Glass
   The cracks only move when the wall is hit. A light first knock makes
   a hairline, then four hits drive the cracks further. Each crack is a
   random walk that keeps its heading and forks now and then. It is
   thick near the impact and thins out toward the tip. Round cracks
   join the long ones, the center turns to white powder, and on the
   last hit the center pieces drop out under gravity.

5. Dust
   Every hit shakes loose a puff of dust, powder keeps falling off the
   broken edge, and the butterflies shake glitter from their wings that
   drifts and settles.

6. Letters
   Every point is projected onto a grid of letters. Far points are drawn
   first and near ones paint over them. Brighter spots get denser
   letters (from 1 i l up to 8 B $ #). Cracks get / | \ - _ in the
   direction they run.

Things you can try (for yourself)
   GATE        where the wall breaks
   THUDS       when it is hit
   N           how many long cracks
   TAG_COLOR   the colours
   CELL        letter size
