HOW IT WORKS - Gnome
JIN2 · @jin_22ee

The whole artwork is one Python file. A small gnome with a lantern peeks
out from the right edge, comes over to say hello, then turns round and
walks off into the snow. Every part of it is made of letters.

1. A real 3D figure
   The coat, head, nose, mittens, boots and pompom are egg shapes covered
   in evenly spaced points. The beard is a curved sheet wrapped round the
   front, and the hat is a tall cone built from rings around a bent spine.
   Because it is 3D, the gnome can turn all the way round.

2. Letters
   Each point is projected onto a grid of letters. Far points are drawn
   first and near ones paint over them. Brighter spots get denser letters
   (from 1 i l up to 8 B $ #), and the letters inside keep changing.
   Edges get / | \ - _ so the outline reads like a drawing.

3. The coat pattern
   A white knit pattern (a line, a zigzag, a row of dots) is wrapped all
   the way round the coat, so it shows from the front, side and back.

4. The lantern
   A box with a roof, posts and a candle flame hangs from one hand and
   swings with every step. Around it is a warm halo, a pool of light on
   the snow, and anything close to it gets brighter. The flame flickers.

5. Motion
   It slides out, slows down before it stops and turns its head to look
   at you. It walks toward you, so it grows, waves, glances left and
   right, then turns round and walks away along a soft curve, getting
   smaller. Its steps slow down together with its body, and the hat tip
   swings as it walks.

6. Footprints and snow
   Every step leaves a white footprint turned the way it was walking.
   Snow falls the whole time.

Things you can try (for yourself)
   WAVE, SHY, OUT     when it waves, looks around and turns to leave
   CENTER             where it stops to say hello
   HAT_LEN, HAT_R     the size of the hat
   LS                 the size of the lantern
   TAG_COLOR          the colours
