HOW IT WORKS - Watching
JIN2 · @jin_22ee

The whole artwork is one Python file. An eye in the dark opens at once,
looks around, and then more eyes appear until they all look at you.
Every part of it is made of letters.

1. One eye is one small description
   Each eye has a position, a size, a lid shape and a list of times:
   when it opens, when it blinks, where the pupil looks and how much the
   whole eye grows. The picture of the eye is worked out from that list
   for every frame.

2. The lid
   The eye is an almond cut out of the letter grid. Each corner has its
   own sharpness, the upper and lower lid have their own curve, and the
   outer corner can lean up or down. Eleven shapes are ready, so no two
   eyes look the same. A random grain on the edge keeps the lid rough.

3. Eyeball, iris and lashes
   The white of the eye is grey and gets darker toward the lids, with
   grain. The iris is black with a grey rim that is brighter along the
   bottom, and two small highlights sit on it. A few + signs outside the
   lids are the lashes. A shut eye is only a dashed line and some lashes.

4. Looking around
   The pupil never glides. It jumps from one spot to the next at random
   short intervals, and a tiny tremor keeps the eye alive. Blinks take
   six frames and close in quarter steps.

5. Cuts
   The film is a list of short shots that overlap in time. Before a shot
   is placed, the code checks that the new eye does not touch any eye
   that is on screen at the same time, and that it is far from the last
   one. If there is no room, the eye comes in smaller.

6. Many eyes
   The last scene places thirteen sizes of eye on the screen without any
   overlap, with room left for them to grow. They appear one by one,
   look around, and at the end all of them look straight at you, grow in
   two jolts and stop blinking.

7. Glitch
   After the first cut, a few frames now and then flicker or have rows
   that slip sideways. It stays off for half a second after every cut so
   a new eye always opens cleanly.

Things you can try (for yourself)
   SHAPES             add your own eye shape
   SIZES              how many eyes there are in the end and how big
   GROW               how much they swell when they find you
   TAG_COLOR          the colours
   shot(...)          start, end, position, size, shape of one eye
