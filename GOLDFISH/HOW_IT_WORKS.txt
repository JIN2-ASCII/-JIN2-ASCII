HOW IT WORKS - Goldfish
JIN2 · @jin_22ee

The whole artwork is one Python file. Six goldfish swim through dark
water. One of them stops for a moment to look at you, then swims on.
Every part of it is made of letters.

1. A real 3D fish
   The side outline of a goldfish is written down as points: the back,
   the round belly, the high dorsal fin, the two-lobed tail. The body is
   that outline swept round in ellipses, so it is truly round and can be
   seen from the side, from the front and from any angle in between.
   The gill cover is a separate part, raised a little above the body.

2. Scales and colour
   Rows of scales are offset like real ones, each brighter at its edge.
   The back is deep red, the flank orange and gold, the belly pale.

3. Fins and tail
   Each fin is a filled shape with thin rays fanning out from the root.
   Near the body it is solid; toward the edge it thins out letter by
   letter until it disappears. The tail follows the body's swing a beat
   later, and its tips flutter. The side fins open away from the body
   and paddle while the fish swims.

4. Eyes and mouth
   The eyes bulge out to the sides and turn slightly forward, so both
   show when a fish faces you. The mouth is a ring of lips that opens
   and closes.

5. Light
   The light comes from the upper left. Each point of the body knows
   which way it faces, so the side toward the light is brighter, and
   the edges of the scales catch a highlight. Now and then a single
   scale flashes like a little star.

6. Motion
   Each fish follows its own path through the water, near and far, with
   its own pace: one glides, one darts and pauses, small ones drift far
   behind. They turn smoothly toward where they are going, and the tail
   beats faster when they speed up. Bubbles rise the whole time.

7. Letters
   Every point is projected onto a grid of letters. Far points are drawn
   first and near ones paint over them. Brighter spots get denser
   letters (from 1 i l up to 8 B $ #), and the letters inside keep
   changing. Edges get / | \ - _ so the outline reads like a drawing.

Things you can try (for yourself)
   FISH        sizes and paths of the six fish (time, x, y, z)
   LIGHT       where the light comes from
   TAG_COLOR   the colours
   CELL        letter size
